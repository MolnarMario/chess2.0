⚙ Board Setup

Fischer Random Chess (Chess960) shuffles the back-rank pieces into one of 960 legal starting setups — bishops always land on opposite-colored squares and the king always starts between the two rooks, so castling still works. It removes memorized opening theory since every game starts from a fresh position.

Capture Values

  • ♙Pawn = 1    ♘♗ = 3    ♖= 5    ♕= 10

Evolution Paths

  • ♙Pawn 2 pts → Knight or Bishop (your choice)
  • ♘Knight / ♗ Bishop 6 pts → Rook
  • ♖Rook 10 pts → Queen
  • ♕Queen 20 pts → ⭐ Amazon (Queen + Knight moves)
  • ♔King — never evolves
  • Leftover points carry over after evolving. The counter on each piece shows progress.

🎲 Gambling

  • Toggle Gamble any time — it applies from your next capture.
  • When on, any capture lets you gamble: a 50/50 roll.
  • Win → double the points. Lose → your capturing piece is destroyed for nothing.
  • Kings never gamble, and you can't risk a piece that's shielding your own King.

⏱ Clock

  • Set equal times, or uneven ones for a handicap. Increments can be equal or lopsided too.
  • Fischer adds the full increment every move; Bronstein only banks back the time you actually used (never more than the increment).
  • Increments can apply from move 1, or only once a clock falls under a chosen threshold.
  • Running out of time loses — unless your opponent has no material left to checkmate you, which is a draw.
